What VaakShastra does

An end-to-end AI pipeline that transforms complex legal documents into actionable intelligence

📄

PDF ingestion & OCR

Upload any court document - scanned or digital. PyMuPDF extracts text with OCR fallback for legacy documents up to 200 pages.

🔍

Semantic case retrieval

InLegalBERT embeddings + GPU-accelerated FAISS index retrieves the 5 most relevant past judgments from the eCourts corpus in milliseconds.

🧠

RAG-powered analysis

LLaMA-3 8B fine-tuned with QLoRA on Indian legal data generates structured analysis - key facts, applicable sections, and verdict prediction.

⚡

GPU-optimized inference

BF16 precision with FlashAttention-2 on NVIDIA H200. 32K context window handles even the longest Supreme Court judgments.

🌐

Multilingual output

IndicTrans2 translates analysis into Hindi, Kannada, Telugu, and Tamil - making legal AI accessible to 800M+ Indian language speakers.

📊

Confidence scoring

Every prediction includes a calibrated confidence percentage based on the strength and relevance of retrieved precedent cases.
